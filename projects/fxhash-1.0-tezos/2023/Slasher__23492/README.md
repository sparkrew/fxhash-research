Slasher

Hommage to Bridget Riley. I wanted to pay tribute to a great artist so wrote an algorithm to generate her style of of diagonal paintings. Her pieces are well thought out and color choices premediated. 

It is basically a two tile tessellation. In my algorithm I turn alot of decisions over to the computer: diagonal orientation, tile placement/displacement and color fill - random solid or gradient. 

Enjoy.
