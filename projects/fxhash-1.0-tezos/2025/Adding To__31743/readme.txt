# Artist Statement
Each iteration is made by accumulating small changes and shown in series of cells. The lines are contained in shapes, and sometimes overflow. 

This is an exercise in process over composition. The grid gives structure for marking progress, but the work isn’t about precision. It’s about rhythm. Growing, filling, containment and breaking loose.
