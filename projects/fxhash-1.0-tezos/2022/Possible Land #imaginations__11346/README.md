Possible Land #imaginations

Possible Land is a series of digital work by creator Yipin Huang who participated in the WAGIWAGI Art Labs, an architectural project《瓦集瓦籍 WAGIWAGI》powered by SUAVEART, Taiwan.

What is WAGIWAGI Art Labs?
WAGIWAGI Art Labs is an architectural project under a bigger picture ”WAGIWAGI”, which tries to use diverse and non-human view point to explore the ecological system of the island, including a number of artist's responses to natural environmental issues such as sun, plant and land, as well as the design of the infrastructure within the architectural space.

The Art Labs, a 300 square meter building is going to be built by Taiwan-based SUAVEART and Indonesian partner Jatiwangi Art Factory in Majalengka, Java, will serve as a cultural center between Taiwan and Indonesia. It could provide studios, temporary offices, and guest house for artists, partners, and researchers.

It will be a joint collaboration designed by Taiwan and constructed with local techniques using bamboo, wood and brick as the main materials, while responding to the current low carbon footprint. The construction process will be presented simultaneously in Indonesia and at Document 15 in Kassel, Germany, in 2022.

WAGIWAGI Art Labs naming:
“WAGIWAGI”, takes our Sun, Plant and Land as context. In Siraya, Atayal, and Indonesian calendar, “WAGI” means sun, also “What a great idea!”. When it is repeated twice, it stands for the Greeting to the nature.
“WA (瓦)” means Tile in Chinese, which stands as the material between nature and human. The first “GI (集)” means Collective, co-working; the second “GI (籍)” represents the Belonging and identy.

Creator Yipin Huang was invited to participate in the early brainstorming of WAGIWAGI Art Labs. The team discussed lots about what should be in the Art Labs? The expected inclusion of functions such as Archive/Library, Fabrication lab, Greenhouse, Kitchen, and Living area, and all projects the imagination of future life and co-governance. Through the various blocks falling from the air, they are planted with possibility and explorations. We are looking forward to the completion of an environmentally friendly and culturally friendly place.

Possible Land #imaginations

Possible Land 是創作者黃宜品參與細着藝術《瓦集瓦籍》之 WAGIWAGI Art Labs 建築空間計劃的數位作品。

WAGIWAGI Art Labs 是什麼：

WAGIWAGI Art Labs 是細着藝術於《瓦集瓦籍》的建築空間計劃。《瓦集瓦籍》嘗試從非人類中心的視角探討島嶼生態系統，其中包含多組藝術家對陽光、植物和土地等自然環境議題回應的創作以及建築空間內的基礎系統設計。

來自台灣的細着藝術與印尼的 Jatiwangi Art Factory 正在爪哇島 Majalengka 建造約 300 平方米的 Art Labs，將作為台灣印尼兩地的文化交流中心，提供藝術工作者、合作夥伴之工作室、臨時辦事處、研究人員暫住地。結合台灣設計，取自當地以竹子、木材及磚瓦為主材料的工法建造同時，回應當前的低碳排放。建造過程將於 2022 年在印尼和德國卡塞爾文件展同步亮相。

WAGIWAGI Art Labs 命名由來：

台灣原住民西拉雅平埔族和泰雅族語中的《Wagi》代表太陽，Wagiwagi 代表向自然打招呼之意，同時亦有What a great idea！的含義。中文翻譯《瓦》代表瓦片，乘載自然與人邊界的媒材；前者《集》則有集體、協作之意；後者《籍》呼應著萬物皆有所屬。

Possible Land 創作：

回應《瓦集瓦籍》的建築空間計劃，創作者黃宜品受邀參與 WAGIWAGI Art Labs 前期發想，團隊多次討論 Art Labs 應該包含何種功能？期望納入的圖書室 (Archive/Library)、數位製造實驗室 (Fabrication lab)、花圃 (Greenhouse)、廚房 (Kitchen)、生活區 (Living area) 等功能，皆投射到未來生活和共同治理的想像，透過畫面中從半空中掉落的各式方塊，落植於一個有可能性及探索性的陌生土地上。

本作品在不同時段觀賞作品時，可見天空顏色的變化。
