# Bézier Aquarium 🦑
## Generative Squids

A window to an unknown minimalist marine world, made mainly with Bézier curves and simple geometric figures. Seaweed, jellyfish, and squid under the ocean. 

Features:

- 15 Color Palettes 

- 7 Background Textures

- 2 Species

Made with p5.js
https://p5js.org/es/
